ABOUT BLOODCONNECT

Connecting people.
Saving lives.

BloodConnect makes blood donation information easier to access and helps connect donors with opportunities to make a difference.

What is BloodConnect?

BloodConnect is a web-based information system designed to make information about blood donation across North Macedonia more accessible and organized. It brings important blood donation information together in one place, helping people find the information they need before donating blood.

Our Moto

A little time of yours, a lifetime for someone else.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage voluntary blood donation by providing clear, accessible and useful information to potential and existing blood donors.

BloodConnect aims to make it easier for people to understand blood donation, check whether they are eligible to donate, discover donation locations and stay informed about current blood needs and campaigns.
